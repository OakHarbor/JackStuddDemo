Additional Service

Three Builders Bidding the Same House

You can't compare bids that aren't bidding the same thing. Most people hand builders a set of plans and a lot of open questions: site work, driveway, septic, well, engineering, allowances, system choices. Every builder answers those differently, and the bids come back too far apart to mean anything.

This closes those questions first. I coordinate the surveyors, engineers, and whoever else your project needs, and you hire them directly and own the work. You end up with a full set of construction documents that eliminates as many variables as possible.
