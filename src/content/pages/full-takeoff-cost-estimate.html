Main Service

Get Your Own Numbers Before You Talk to Builders

You own the land and you have plans. Before you talk to builders, you get your own numbers. I count everything that goes into your house, down to how many 2x6s are in it, solicit real bids from subs across every trade, price the materials, and set your allowances.

That's a full takeoff and cost estimate, built the same way a builder builds one, and you own all of it. Instead of walking into those meetings on defense, you walk in on offense, with a subject matter expert on your side who knows what the builder knows.

Includes analyzing and comparing up to three builder bids.

Who It's For

Someone with land and plans is a takeoff and estimate. Your plans can come from an architect or a stock set.
