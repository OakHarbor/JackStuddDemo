My Story

The Piece That Carries the Load

About Me

I'm Justin Davis. I live in Boone and I've spent the last decade building custom homes in the High Country for other people's companies. Project manager, superintendent, on site.

I got into this because of a question I couldn't get past: if someone I love were building a house up here, who would I send them to? I know this market and I know the people in it, and I couldn't come up with an answer I was comfortable with. Not because there aren't good builders. Because the thing I'd want for my own family, someone who holds a real standard and stays on it every day, isn't something you can just go buy. So I built it.

My Company

Jack Stud LLC. A jack stud is the piece that carries the header over an opening. It takes the load so the opening can be there at all. Every wide window and every wall of glass looking out at these mountains has one working next to it, buried in the wall, doing the structural work nobody sees. That's the job. You get the house. I carry the load that makes it possible.

I work for owners, not builders. Depending on what you need, that means running your entire project while you hold the permit, representing you against a builder you've already hired, or getting your project defined and priced before anyone bids it. I don't take a cut of the build, and I don't make more money when your house costs more. You work with me, not with a company that hands you off.

Explore Owner-Builder Consulting

Philosophy

I work for the owner and nobody else. My fee doesn't move with what the house costs, so I have nothing to gain from any decision except the right one. And because I'm working for you, you're in the decisions with me. You'll understand your own project well enough to know what you're looking at.

The rest is standards. A house is only as good as the standard the person running it is willing to hold. I set expectations clearly before anyone starts and I hold everyone on the job to them, every day, on every trade.

Experience

Over ten years building custom homes in the High Country. Four companies, ranging from the largest and most established builder in the area to small shops. High-end new construction start to finish, from pre-construction through closeout.

That's project management and superintendent work in this market specifically: Boone, Banner Elk, Blowing Rock. I hold a North Carolina general contractor's license. I know what things cost here, which subs do good work, what the terrain does to a budget, and where projects go sideways.
