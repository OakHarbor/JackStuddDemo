Owner-Builder Consulting

What It Is + Why

North Carolina allows a homeowner to pull the building permit on their own home. You own the land, you pull the permits, and you're involved throughout the build. That's the owner-builder exemption, and most people never hear about it because nobody in the industry is incentivised to bring it up. The contracts are yours. The money is yours. The project is yours.

Making sure everything runs smoothly is mine. I use my connections to get bids for all phases of construction, and my experience to put together a full takeoff and cost estimate. I put in the orders. I build the schedule and keep the project on track. On site, I'm your eyes and ears. Nothing moves forward without you knowing exactly what's happening and why.

Nothing on your project moves my fee in either direction. I have no reason to talk you into spending more than you need to, and no reason to cut a corner to save a dollar, because neither one adds anything to my bottom line. Every recommendation I make is just what I'd do if it were my house.

That's what you're getting here. Someone who knows this work, on your project every day, with nothing pulling at my judgment. I've spent a decade plus doing this, and this is the way I believe it delivers the most for the homeowner. You get the same build, for less, with nobody's interests sitting opposite yours.

Who It's For

This is for people who look hard at what they're buying.

You want your money going into the house. Into the trades doing the work, the materials going into the walls, and the person making sure all of it comes together the way it should. Every dollar you spend should be buying something that makes the house better.

That's what this is. A straight line between you and the people building your house, with someone who knows the work standing in it with you.

What's Included + How It Works

A subject matter expert on your project, start to finish, for a flat monthly fee. From pre-construction coordination through moving your furniture in, I'm setting up, facilitating, and keeping every aspect of the build on track.

  • 01

    Defining the Project

    Everything that has to be settled before your house can be priced or permitted. Survey, site plan, engineering, septic and well, the systems and allowances that plans leave open. I line up the surveyors, engineers, and specialists your project calls for, and they contract with you directly so the work belongs to you.

  • 02

    Pricing the House

    A complete takeoff and cost estimate. I count every piece that goes into your house, bid the work out across every trade, price the materials and pick the vendors. You end up with a line by line account of what your home costs to build. The schedule gets built from it, so you know the order of the work and when each part of it needs to happen.

  • 03

    Building It

    This is where your house comes out of the ground. Somebody is there every day who knows what good work looks like, and your trades show up in the right order to work that's ready for them. I track your budget against the estimate and your schedule against the plan. You'll need to be at the inspections, and those get scheduled around you.

  • 04

    Handing It Over

    A complete record of your house. Every sub, every vendor, every warranty and guarantee that went into it, documented and handed to you. When something needs servicing, you know who touched it and how to reach them. And I'm available after the build if you'd rather have help than handle it yourself.

Every build needs some version of all four. Yours will have its own requirements, and some of what's above won't apply at all. What your project actually takes is the first conversation we have, and everything above gets shaped around it.
