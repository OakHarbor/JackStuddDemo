Main Service

A Different Way to Build

You hold the contracts and fund the work. You have final say on all decisions. I handle the schedule, the subs, the sequencing, and the site, start to finish, for a flat monthly fee instead of a percentage of what your house costs.

It isn't right for everyone, but for the right client it means real control, real transparency, and significant savings over the traditional route.

Who It's For

People who want to understand their own project instead of handing it off. You want to be in the decisions, you want to know what things actually cost and why, and you see the value in having a subject matter expert on your side.

It isn't for anyone whose priority is building as cheaply as possible. What I do is get a high-quality home built at better than GC pricing.

Why Build This Way

Hiring a GC Isn't the Only Option

Almost everyone up here builds the same way: hire a GC, sign a contract, hope it goes well. That works for some people. It isn't the only option, and it isn't always the best one.

I offer real alternatives, up to and including building without a GC at all, and I help you figure out which path actually fits you.
